Request deletion of your Bubble Quest data

Use this form to ask Le Labs to delete the data Bubble Quest stores about you. You do not need to reinstall the app. You can also delete your data inside the app under Settings → Backup → Delete Account & Cloud Data.

What is deleted: your cloud-saved profile and stats, your backup and recovery code, your leaderboard entry, your notification token, and your anonymous account and any linked Google account. Nothing is kept afterwards, except routine Firebase backups that expire within 90 days.

We will check that the request is yours by replying to the email address you give below, then delete your data within 30 days. See the privacy policy for details.
