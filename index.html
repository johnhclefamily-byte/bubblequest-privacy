Bubble Quest Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Bubble Quest ("the app") is a bubble-shooter game published by Le Labs. This policy explains what information the app handles, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Our Terms of Use cover the rules for playing, purchases and rewarded ads.

Information we collect

  • Anonymous account ID. The app signs you in anonymously using Firebase Authentication. This creates a random ID; no name, email or password is required to play.
  • Game progress and stats. Your levels, scores, purchases of in-game items, and profile statistics (for example levels played and stars earned) are stored on your device and backed up to Google Firebase Firestore, linked to your anonymous ID. A backup of your save file can also be restored using a recovery code shown in the app.
  • Optional Google account link. If you choose to link a Google account, Firebase Authentication receives your Google account ID, email address and name so your progress can follow you to another device. Linking is optional.
  • Leaderboard entry. If you link a Google account and appear on the leaderboard, your display name, points, stars and levels completed are stored and are visible to other players.
  • Notification token. If you allow notifications, a Firebase Cloud Messaging token identifies your device so we can send you reminders and event announcements. You can turn these off in the app's Settings or in your device settings.
  • Advertising data. The app can show optional rewarded video ads (see "Ads" below). To do this, Google AdMob and its ad partners collect and use your device's advertising ID, along with device information, IP address and approximate location, and ad interaction data.
  • Purchase information. If you buy coins or other items, the purchase is handled by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase happened, such as a purchase token and the item bought, but not your card or payment details.

Information we do not collect

  • No contact list, photos or files from your device.
  • No precise location. Ad partners may infer approximate location from your IP address.
  • No payment card details. Google Play handles payment.

How information is used

  • To save your progress, run the leaderboard, send notifications you allow, and keep the app working.
  • To show ads, and to measure and prevent fraud in how ads are served.
  • To process purchases and give you the items you buy.

We do not sell your personal information.

Ads

Bubble Quest may offer rewarded video ads: you can choose to watch an ad in return for an in-game reward. Ads are served by Google AdMob. We may also use other ad networks through AdMob mediation, and if we do, they will be named in this policy before they are used. Ad providers may use your advertising ID to show ads and to personalise them where the law allows. Watching an ad is always your choice. When you watch one, the app keeps a count on your device of how many ads you watched that day, so daily limits can be applied.

  • Your choices on Android. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Settings (usually under Google > Ads).
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland. We ask for your consent before ads that use personal data are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the "Ad privacy choices" button in the app's Settings.
  • US states and other regions. If your state or country gives you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data for ads, you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your device's settings (usually Google > Ads), or use the privacy choices option in the app where it is shown. You can also ask us to delete your data using the form below.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging) stores and processes account, progress and notification data, under Google's privacy terms.
  • Google AdMob provides ads, under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play processes purchases, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Delete your data

To request deletion of the data Bubble Quest (Le Labs) stores about you:

  1. Fill in the data deletion request form, or email lelabs.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Bubble Quest data deletion".
  2. Include the display name you used on the leaderboard (if any) and the email of the Google account you linked (if any). We reply to your email to confirm the request is yours.
  3. We will delete your request's data within 30 days.

What is deleted: your cloud-saved profile and stats, your backup and recovery code, your leaderboard entry, your notification token, and the association with your anonymous account ID and any linked Google account.

What is kept: nothing is kept after deletion. Routine Firebase backups may hold copies for up to 90 days before they expire.

Data held by Google as an ad or payment provider is governed by Google's policies. You can manage it at myaccount.google.com. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

Data retention

Data is kept while your account is in use, or until you request deletion.

Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest revision. If we add a new ad network or change how data is used, we will update this page before the change takes effect.
