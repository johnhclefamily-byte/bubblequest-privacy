Bubble Quest Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Bubble Quest ("the app") is a bubble-shooter game published by Le Labs. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

  • Anonymous account ID. The app signs you in anonymously using Firebase Authentication. This creates a random ID; no name, email or password is required.
  • Game progress and stats. Your levels, scores, and profile statistics are stored on your device and backed up to Google Firebase Firestore, linked to your anonymous ID.
  • Leaderboard entry. If you submit an Endless-mode score, your score and the display name you choose (up to 20 characters) are stored and are visible to other players.

Information we do not collect

  • No real name, email address, phone number, or contact list.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising ID, and the app shows no ads.
  • No payment information.

How information is used

Solely to save your progress, run the leaderboard, and keep the app working. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Service providers

Data is stored and processed by Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) under Google's privacy terms.

Delete your data

To request deletion of the data Bubble Quest (Le Labs) stores about you:

  1. Email lelabs.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Bubble Quest data deletion".
  2. Include the display name you used on the leaderboard (if any).
  3. We will delete your request's data within 30 days.

What is deleted: your cloud-saved profile and stats, your leaderboard entry (display name and score), and the association with your anonymous account ID.

What is kept: nothing is kept after deletion. Routine Firebase backups may hold copies for up to 90 days before they expire.

Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

Data retention

Data is kept while your anonymous account is in use, or until you request deletion.

Children

The app is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It does not ask for any personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest revision.
